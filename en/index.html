Explore all features

Add videos

Select individual files or add an entire folder, with the option to include subfolders. FrameCatalog keeps the original files in place, reads details such as duration and resolution, and generates thumbnails.

FrameCatalog can read recording dates and times when they are available in the video metadata. The import results show which files could not be added.

Preview videos

Hover over a thumbnail to play a short, silent preview. Open the original in your usual player when you want to watch the whole video.

You can replace an automatically generated thumbnail. Enter a time or move the slider, check the frame, and choose the scene you want to use.

Organize your library

Use tags and five-star ratings, and create your own text, date, or choice fields. Batch editing lets you update the same information across multiple videos.

Keep aliases for each performer or provider together. Give performers their own ratings and favorite ranks, group their videos, and record details such as clothing separately for each video.

See what is in your library

View statistics for your entire library or just the filtered results. Check video and performer counts, total duration, and source file size. Select a performer or tag in the breakdown to see the matching videos.

Search and filter

Search by file name or entered information. Combine tags, ratings, recording date ranges, resolution, duration, and folders. Match all selected tags with AND, or any of them with OR.

Find videos by a performer’s name or alias. Name and save the filters you use often so you can apply them again later.

Back up and restore

Back up your library entries, custom fields, thumbnails, previews, and display settings together, and restore them when needed. Back up your original video files separately.

BEFORE YOU START

System and video requirements

For Windows 10 / 11 PCs

FrameCatalog supports 64-bit (x64) Windows PCs. Switch the app interface between English and Japanese.

Library information, thumbnails, and previews are stored on your PC. Larger collections take longer to add and require free disk space for previews and other generated files.

H.265 requires HEVC support in Windows

Windows must support H.265 (HEVC) to generate thumbnails and previews from HEVC videos. Depending on your setup, you may need to install HEVC Video Extensions separately from Microsoft Store. The extension may require a separate purchase.

Video compatibility depends on the internal codec and profile, as well as your PC’s capabilities, not just the file extension. MPEG-1, MPEG-2, and MPEG-4 Visual use bundled video processing components. H.264, H.265 (HEVC), and VC-1/WMV9 use Windows video features. Support for every video format is not guaranteed. Before purchasing the upgrade, use the free version to test importing, thumbnails, and previews with your own videos.

Adding a video does not convert or overwrite the original file. Previews are short and silent. Click Play to open the full video in your usual player.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Questions about organizing your local video library

Do I need to move my video files?

No. Register videos from your PC or external drives and manage them in their original locations.

How is this different from organizing folders?

Find videos across folders using ratings, tags, performers and custom fields. Combine AND or OR tag filters with resolution, duration and recording date.

Can I preview videos without opening each file?

Hover over a thumbnail to play a short, silent preview. For full-length playback, the play button opens your usual video player.

Can I try it for free?

Add up to 50 videos for free and test thumbnails and previews with your own files. A one-time purchase removes the library size limit. There is no monthly subscription.

Where can I get FrameCatalog?

Download it from Microsoft Store for a 64-bit (x64) PC running Windows 10 or 11.
