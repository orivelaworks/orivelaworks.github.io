Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Orivela Works (“we,” “us,” or “our”) explains below how information is handled by FrameCatalog and this website.

FrameCatalog stores your library information on your PC. The app does not include a feature that automatically uploads your registered videos or library information to our servers.

1. Information processed by the app

To register, display, search, play, edit, and back up your library, the app processes videos you select and the following information:

  • Video file names, locations, sizes, duration, resolution, codecs, and similar file information
  • Thumbnails, previews, and file identification information generated from videos
  • Titles, performer names, providers, aliases, notes, categories, tags, ratings, and other information you enter
  • App settings and Microsoft Store purchase license information

Videos, file names, locations, and information you enter may contain personally identifiable information. Register only the information you need, and handle other people’s information appropriately.

2. Storage and backups

The library database, thumbnails, previews, settings, and similar data are stored in the app’s data folders on your PC. Original videos remain in the locations you specify.

Backups are saved to a location you choose. They contain library information and images, but not the original video files. If you choose a shared or cloud-synced folder, the data may be accessible to others or synced outside your PC according to that service’s settings.

3. Microsoft Store and external services

The paid upgrade that removes the video limit uses Microsoft Store to retrieve product information, process purchases, and verify purchased licenses. Microsoft handles payment and Microsoft account processing. FrameCatalog does not obtain your card number or Microsoft account password.

The app uses Microsoft Edge WebView2 to display its interface. For information about usage and diagnostic data handled by Microsoft Store, Windows, WebView2, and related services, refer to their settings and the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

4. Advertising and analytics

We have not added our own advertising or behavioral tracking tools to FrameCatalog or this website. Our website hosting provider may process connection information, such as IP addresses and access times, to deliver pages and maintain security.

5. Support inquiries

If you contact us by email, we use your email address, name (if provided), message, attachments, and similar information to respond and investigate problems. We use Google’s Gmail to send and receive email. See the Google Privacy Policy for details of Google’s processing.

We retain inquiry information for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and delete information that is no longer needed. We do not sell it or use it for advertising beyond the stated purpose. Except where required by law, we do not share it with third parties who are not needed to handle the inquiry.

6. Managing and deleting information

You can remove library entries within the app. Removing an entry does not delete the original video or previously created backups. You are responsible for managing and deleting those files. Microsoft Store manages your purchase history.

To ask about accessing, correcting, or deleting information you have sent us by email, contact us at the address below. We may need to verify your identity.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app’s features or our services change. The updated policy and revision date will be available on this page.
